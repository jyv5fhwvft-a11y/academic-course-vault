🎪 Checkout Lane02 / 04

Algorithmic Queue Analogies

FCFS: Strict checkout queue order. A heavy process cart at the front delays a single item customer behind it indefinitely (The Convoy Effect).

SJF: Shaking up the queue to let the small item customers pass first. Minimizes mathematical average wait times perfectly.

Round Robin: Force-pausing checkout execution every 30 seconds (Time Quantum) to spin slow tenants to the back of the line.

🔍 Metric Ledger03 / 04

Preemption and Time Slices

Preemptive systems leverage hardware clock timer interrupts to tear uncooperative software threads off the CPU core lines.

If the Time Quantum is too small, excessive context switches waste cycles. If too large, Round Robin degrades into simple FCFS queues.
