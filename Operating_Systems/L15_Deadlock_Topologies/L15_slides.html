🎪 Circular Wait02 / 04

The Dining Philosophers Dilemma

Five thinkers sit around a table with 5 total forks. To eat, each must claim both their left and right forks. If everyone grabs their left fork simultaneously, the system hits a hard **Hold and Wait** gridlock.

Since no philosopher can forcefully steal an adjacent resource (**No Preemption**), everyone starves staring at their plate. This permanent lock freeze is a **Deadlock**.

🔍 Coffman Quartet03 / 04

The Four Coffman Conditions

A deadlock cannot manifest unless all four traits are present at once:

• Mutual Exclusion: Resources are held in an unshareable, exclusive mode.

• Hold & Wait: Processes retain existing locks while queuing for additional items.

• No Preemption: Allocated tokens cannot be stolen away mid-sentence.

• Circular Wait: A closed loop cycle of thread dependencies exists.

Mapping a Deterministic Deadlock

How recursive cross-account transfer paths trigger a permanent hardware level lock inversion trap:

Total system gridlock.
